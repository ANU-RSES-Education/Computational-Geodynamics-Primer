# Geodynamics Applications

This part applies the fluid and solid mechanics of the earlier parts to the
Earth: how the lithosphere breaks, and how the mantle beneath it moves heat.

- [Stress, faults and the Mohr circle](stress-and-faulting.md) finds the
  stress on any plane through the rock, uses the Coulomb criterion to decide
  when that plane slips, and explains Anderson's three classes of fault.

- [Rayleigh–Bénard convection](rayleigh-benard-convection.md) takes a layer
  heated from below as the simplest analogue of the mantle: when it starts to
  convect, and how its heat flow scales with the Rayleigh number, tested with
  an Underworld3 model you can run in the browser.
