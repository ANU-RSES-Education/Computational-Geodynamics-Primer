# Mathematical Fundamentals

The equations in this book express a small number of physical principles:
mass, momentum and energy are conserved as material moves and deforms. This
part sets out how those principles are written as equations for a continuum.

- [Conservation laws](conservation-laws.md) derives the conservation of mass
  and of heat energy for a small volume of material, and introduces the
  vector and index notation used in the rest of the book.

Still to come: tensor notation, vector calculus identities and coordinate
systems.
